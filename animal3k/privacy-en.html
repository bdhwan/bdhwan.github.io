Privacy Policy — Animal Three Kingdoms (동물삼국지)

Effective: November 29, 2026

Operator: Altamirasoft (알타미라소프트) · Contact: bdhwan@gmail.com

1. Information we collect directly

Animal Three Kingdoms (the "App") has no sign-up, login or payments. The operator does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email or phone number, and the App has no operator server.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The App is free. It uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) by Google LLC to show rewarded video ads only when you tap an optional reward button. We do not use banner or interstitial ads. To serve ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device advertising identifiers: Android Advertising ID; iOS IDFA (only if you allow tracking)
  • IP address and the approximate location inferred from it (country / city level)
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and views, and app launch information
  • Device information (model, OS version, language) and diagnostic / performance information

This information is handled under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The operator cannot access it individually and only sees aggregated AdMob reports.

3. Analytics and remote settings (Google Firebase)

To improve the App we use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config by Google LLC.

  • Sent: an app-instance identifier, the Android Advertising ID (on iOS, the IDFA if you allow tracking), approximate region inferred from IP, usage events (app launch, tutorial, stage start and end, rewarded ad views), device information and error / diagnostic information.
  • We do not send names, email addresses or other directly identifying data, and we do not link these events to any account or name (the App has no accounts).
  • These data are configured not to be used for ad personalization.
  • Remote Config downloads game settings such as ad frequency; a device-level identifier may also be sent to Google for this.

4. Personalized ads and your choices

  • iOS: the App asks for tracking permission at first launch. If you decline, the IDFA is not used. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).
  • In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require consent, we use Google's consent tool (UMP). You can change or withdraw your consent under "Privacy options" in the App settings.
  • If you decline personalized ads you may still see ads, but not ads based on your interests. All game features work the same either way.

5. Data stored only on your device

Game progress (nest, resident animals, currencies, upgrades), settings (sound effects) and the daily ad-view count are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the App or tap "Start over" in Settings. The operator cannot access them. Progress cannot be transferred between devices.

6. Notifications

The current version does not send notifications.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network state (Android): to load ads and remote settings.
  • Advertising ID (Android, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): for the ads and analytics above.
  • Tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency): for personalized ads; declining does not limit gameplay.
  • The App does not use location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or storage permissions.

8. Retention

The operator does not separately retain ad or analytics data. Retention of data collected by Google follows Google's policies. Support emails are used only to reply and are deleted after the inquiry is handled.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and ad content is limited to the Teen (T) rating or below. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights and contact

For access or deletion requests or any question, email bdhwan@gmail.com. Because usage events are not linked to you, individual deletion may not be possible, but we will handle requests as far as we can. You can manage data processed by Google at https://myaccount.google.com or through the device settings above.

11. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
